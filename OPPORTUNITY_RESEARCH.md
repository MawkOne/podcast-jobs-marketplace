# Opportunity Research — Non-Red-Ocean Job Board / Marketplace Niches

**Research question:** where does the ytjobs pattern — a two-sided marketplace between creative service providers and content-producing businesses — still have open water?

**Method:** DataForSEO (Google Ads keyword volumes + competition, live SERP saturation checks) + Google small-business data (DataForSEO business listings — GBP-listed business counts per category, US). Pulled 2026-10-14.

**The ytjobs pattern, formalized:** `[content-producing entity under monetization pressure] ↔ [production talent]`, where the demand side publishes content but isn't a media company (YouTube channels, podcasters, streamers, course creators), the supply side is freelance production talent (editors, designers, writers, producers), and the marketplace wins by owning the niche's hiring/search intent. The tell that a niche is ready: **fragmented small agencies + generic job boards in the SERP, and no dedicated marketplace ranking.**

---

## 1. Ranked opportunities

### 🥇 1. Podcast production marketplace — *the closest analog to ytjobs, and the most open*

**Podcasters ↔ podcast editors, producers, show-notes writers, audio engineers.**

| Signal | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Search demand | `podcast jobs` 1,900/mo **LOW** comp · `podcast editor` 1,300/mo · `podcast producer jobs` 880/mo **LOW** · `podcast editing services` 390/mo |
| SERP saturation | **No dedicated marketplace ranks.** "podcast jobs" → podnews.net/jobs (a newsletter's board), entertainmentcareers, LinkedIn, Indeed. "podcast editing services" → Reddit threads, a Buzzsprout listicle, and ~6 small agencies (weeditpodcasts.com, podcastmagician.com, freedompodcasting.com, nextdaypodcast.com, podigy.co) |
| Demand-side density (GBP) | 6,828 podcast studios + 4,733 podcast production businesses listed — plus the long tail of independent podcasters invisible to GBP |
| Structure | **Fragmented small agencies = the pre-marketplace state** — exactly what video editing looked like before ytjobs. The agencies rank individually; nobody owns the category |

**Why it wins:** the demand side (podcasters) has recurring weekly production needs, the supply side (audio editors) is fragmented across agencies and Fiverr, and the SERP is a listicle of agencies — no one has built the talent marketplace. The template in this directory ports directly: swap the role taxonomy (Podcast Editor, Producer, Show Notes Writer, Audio Engineer, Sound Designer) and the landing families (`/hire/podcast-editors`, `/podcast-jobs`).

### 🥈 2. TikTok creator marketplace — *biggest volume, but AI-tool headwinds*

**TikTok brands/creators ↔ short-form editors, creators, strategists.**

| Signal | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Search demand | `tiktok jobs` 6,600/mo **LOW** · `tiktok editor` 3,600/mo **LOW** — the largest underserved volumes found |
| SERP saturation | "tiktok jobs" → corporate TikTok careers pages + generic boards (SERP reads it as jobs *at* TikTok, not *for* TikTok creators). "tiktok editor" → Reddit, YouTube tutorials, and **AI tools (vizard.ai, clideo)** — no human-talent marketplace ranks |
| Risk | The same intent is being attacked by AI editing tools; TikTok's US regulatory situation is a platform risk; discovery for this talent happens partly in-app, not via Google |

**Verdict:** the volume is real and the SERP is empty of marketplaces — but the competitor here isn't another board, it's AI. A play here should position human creators *above* AI tools (strategy, brand voice, on-camera talent) rather than competing on editing throughput.

### 🥉 3. Twitch/streamer creative services — *zero competition, discovery problem*

**Streamers ↔ emote artists, overlay designers, VOD editors, thumbnail designers.**

| Signal | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Search demand | `twitch jobs` 1,600/mo **LOW**; niche keywords tiny (`twitch emote artist` 30/mo, `overlay designer` 10/mo) but transactions are high-value and recurring |
| SERP saturation | "twitch jobs" → corporate Twitch careers + generic boards. **The creator-side is completely unserved** — no marketplace ranks |
| Structural note | Streamers aren't GBP-listed (invisible in business data) and discovery happens on Twitch/Discord, not Google — a marketplace must win in-community, not via SEO |

**Verdict:** genuinely blue ocean, but the acquisition channel is community-driven rather than search-driven — a different go-to-market than the SEO-landing playbook in this template.

### 4. Course-creator / e-learning production — *strong density, needs a saturation check*

**Course creators & e-learning companies ↔ course video editors, instructional designers, slide/motion designers.**

| Signal | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Search demand | `instructional designer jobs` 6,600/mo **LOW** · `online course video editing` 480/mo |
| Demand-side density (GBP) | 12,266 e-learning + 15,009 online-course businesses listed |
| Open question | The instructional-design keyword skews corporate (L&D departments); the creator-side (individual course creators hiring editors) needs a dedicated SERP check before committing |

### 5. Dark horses (density without a marketplace)

| Niche | GBP business count | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Churches** | 143,401 | Every church produces weekly media (sermon video, livestream) and hires media producers — massive density, near-zero dedicated marketplaces, but low willingness-to-pay and local hiring habits |
| **Restaurants** | 777,401 | Food photography/video demand is enormous but hyper-local and word-of-mouth |
| **Event venues** | 40,214 | Same shape as restaurants |

High density, but discovery isn't search-driven for creative hiring — these need a different motion than the SEO playbook.

## 2. Ruled out (red or crowded oceans)

| Niche | Why ruled out |
|---|---|
| **UGC creators for brands** | `ugc creator jobs` 1,900/mo LOW — but the SERP is full of funded dedicated players: billo.app, joinbrands, collabstr, influee, ugcjobs.com, vidovo, backstage. Multiple marketplaces already compete |
| **Voice-over / dubbing** | `voice over jobs` 8,100/mo — Voices.com and Voice123 own the category |
| **Authors / self-publishing** | `book cover designer` 1,000/mo — Reedsy is exactly this marketplace, established |
| **Music production** | `recording studio` 24,724 + `music studio` 31,322 businesses, but SoundBetter (Spotify) covers mixing/production hiring |
| **General freelance / video editing** | Upwork/Fiverr/ytjobs itself — the red ocean this research is avoiding |

## 5. Recommendation

**Build the podcast production marketplace first.** It is the highest-fidelity match to the ytjobs pattern: recurring weekly production demand, fragmented supply currently served by small agencies, zero dedicated marketplace in the SERP, and LOW competition on the category keywords. The template in this directory ports directly — swap the role taxonomy, the landing families, and the catalog sync source.

**Second bet:** TikTok, positioned as human-creator talent *above* the AI tools — with eyes open that AI editing is the real competitor and platform risk is nonzero.

**Watchlist:** course-creator production (run the dedicated saturation check first) and Twitch creative services (blue ocean, but community-driven acquisition — a different go-to-market than this template's SEO playbook).
